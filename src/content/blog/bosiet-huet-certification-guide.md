## Two related, but not identical, certifications

BOSIET (Basic Offshore Safety Induction and Emergency Training) and HUET (Helicopter Underwater
Escape Training) are both OPITO offshore safety standards.

- **BOSIET includes helicopter escape training.** Its objectives include escaping from a helicopter that has ditched in water.
- **HUET also exists as its own OPITO standard.** For example, OPITO publishes "HUET with Emergency Breathing System (EBS)" as a separate product, and lists dozens of approved centres delivering it.

Which one a specific employer or site asks for can differ. *(Needs verification: whether a

## What each one generally focuses on

- **BOSIET** covers broader offshore safety induction: offshore hazards and regulations, helicopter emergencies, sea survival, first aid, and basic fire-fighting with self-rescue in low visibility.
- **HUET** focuses on the practical simulation of a helicopter ditching — learning to escape from a helicopter that has landed in water. It is relevant to anyone who travels to an offshore installation or vessel by helicopter.

## Why employers ask for a specific, current certificate

Offshore employers typically require a currently valid certificate, not simply evidence that the
training was completed at some point in the past. The validity period is set by the OPITO
standard, not by the training centre:

- **BOSIET with EBS:** OPITO states a maximum interval of **4 years** between completing BOSIET and completing the FOET refresher course.
- **HUET with EBS:** OPITO lists a validity of **4 years**.

OPITO also notes that a duty holder, asset owner or operator may state different requirements.
Other variants (such as Compressed Air EBS or Tropical versions) have their own OPITO pages, so
check the exact variant an employer names.

## Questions worth asking a training provider before booking

- Is the centre approved by OPITO for this exact course variant?
- Does the employer need BOSIET, a separate HUET certificate, or both?
- When does my current certificate expire, and which refresher course applies?
