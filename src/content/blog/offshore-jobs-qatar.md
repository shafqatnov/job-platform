## Qatar's oil and gas sector

Qatar's gas industry is anchored by the North Field. QatarEnergy describes the North Gas Field,
discovered in 1971, as "the largest single non-associated gas reservoir in the world", with
commercial production starting in 1991. QatarEnergy also operates offshore oil fields and three
offshore production stations.

Job seekers can therefore come across both offshore roles and the onshore roles that support
them. Which employers are recruiting, and for which roles, changes continuously — this article
describes the kinds of requirements a candidate should expect, not a live hiring snapshot.
*(Needs verification: current employer-level hiring activity, which this article does not
track.)*

## Typical requirement areas

- **Safety training** — OPITO, the body that sets the BOSIET and HUET standards, states that this training is required for offshore personnel travelling by helicopter to offshore installations. See our [BOSIET and HUET: Certification Guide](/blog/bosiet-huet-certification-guide). *(Needs verification: the exact certificates a specific Qatari employer or site requires.)*
- **Work visa and sponsorship** — the Qatar Government Portal states that newcomers who want to work and live in Qatar need a Work Residence Permit, for which they need a Qatari employer (a company or an individual), and that the permit is renewed each year by the employer. QatarEnergy, for example, states that it applies for the work permit and employment entry visa after the candidate clears its checks. *(Needs verification: current documents and timelines — confirm with the employer or Qatar's official authorities, not this article.)*
- **Medical fitness** — QatarEnergy states that it requires a background check and a pre-employment medical test to make sure an applicant is fit to work. Offshore training itself also has medical entry requirements set by OPITO. *(Needs verification: any offshore-specific medical standard a given employer applies.)*

## What this means for job seekers

- Confirm exactly which safety certifications a specific employer requires before assuming any certificate you already hold is sufficient.
- Treat "sponsorship provided" claims in any job listing as something to verify directly with the employer, not as a general guarantee across the industry.
- Be cautious of any unsolicited offer that asks for payment in exchange for a visa, a job offer, or a certification shortcut. *(Needs verification: an official Qatari source on recruitment fraud to cite here.)*
