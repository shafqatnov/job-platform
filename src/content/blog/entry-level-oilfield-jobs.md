## A different hiring path than office roles

Many oilfield and drilling roles are built around supervised, on-the-job skill progression rather
than a university degree — physical fitness, willingness to work rotational schedules, and a clean
safety record often matter more at entry level than a formal qualification. This is a generalization
about how the industry is commonly structured, not a guarantee from any specific employer. *(Needs
