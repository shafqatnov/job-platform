## What BOSIET covers

BOSIET stands for Basic Offshore Safety Induction and Emergency Training. It is an OPITO
standard intended for people who are new to — or returning to — the offshore oil and gas
industry. OPITO states that BOSIET and HUET training is required for offshore personnel who
travel by helicopter to offshore installations.

According to OPITO, the BOSIET (with Emergency Breathing System) course trains learners to:

- identify the hazards specific to offshore installations and how risks are controlled;
- understand key offshore safety regulations and basic safety management;
- prepare for and respond to helicopter emergencies, with particular focus on escaping from a helicopter after it has ditched in water;
- practise sea survival and first aid; and
- use basic fire-fighting equipment and self-rescue techniques in low-visibility conditions.

The course content and guided learning hours are set by the OPITO standard (OPITO lists 21
guided learning hours for BOSIET with EBS). The course is delivered by OPITO-approved training
centres — OPITO reports more than 240 approved centres worldwide — not by Jobnura or any single
employer. Prices are set by each centre. *(Needs verification: pricing — this varies by centre
and is not stated here.)*

## Why employers require it

Offshore sites carry risks that onshore sites generally do not — helicopter transport, emergency
sea survival, and confined, remote working conditions. Employers require proof of a valid BOSIET
certificate (or an equivalent recognized certificate) so that every person on site has already
practised the emergency procedures relevant to that environment before an actual emergency
makes that the wrong time to learn them.

## How long a BOSIET certificate stays valid

OPITO sets the refresher interval in the standard itself. For BOSIET with EBS, OPITO states that
the maximum interval between completing BOSIET and completing the refresher course, Further
Offshore Emergency Training (FOET), is **4 years**.

BOSIET comes in several variants (for example with EBS, with Compressed Air EBS, or the Tropical
version), and each has its own OPITO page. An operator or asset owner may also set its own
requirements. Always check the exact variant an employer asks for.

## Where to find an accredited training provider

OPITO's online standards library lists the approved centres that deliver each standard. Check
that a centre is listed by OPITO for the exact course variant before booking.
